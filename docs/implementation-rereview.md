### High 1 · Financial support

**Baseline.** An ordinary Microsoft case accepted a $12.34 dividend per share with only a quarterly revenue quotation. **Checkpoint.** The same unsupported assertion produced zero inbox rows. Separate edits inventing fee income, a growth rate and a dividend in other displayed fields were rejected. A genuine USD 28 million → USD 35 million comparison then reached the inbox with a USD 7 million difference (+25%). With only revenue available, an explicitly declared missing dividend supported an honest partial.

The worker now selects evidence, calculations, gaps and status. The host generates the displayed assertions, checks them again at submission, and displays metric, issuer, period, basis and units beside numeric citations. This closes the fabricated-prose failure. It verifies a bounded accounting method. Whether those grounded figures answer the question is a separate failure demonstrated under #7. ### High 2 · Gap lifecycle

**Baseline.** A worker that found revenue could not declare a missing dividend. An initially empty search also left a permanent gap after successful capture and rereading. **Checkpoint.** The dividend gap now carries its attempted search, metric, period and remedy. Empty search → mocked SEC capture → eligible reread produced a complete brief for the recovered value of 120 USD. Tests also verify that a wrong issuer, role, quarter, unit or provider cannot close the corresponding requirement.

**Remaining failures.** With quarterly values of 80 and 120 USD, submitting the earlier value as the current operand created an invalid-input comparison gap. Correcting the order produced a valid +40 USD (+50%) calculation. The service still rejected a complete brief and accepted a partial saying the comparison could not be computed. The resolution key includes the failed attempt’s ordered pair. In a synthetic 17-result corpus, the only eligible dividend was result 17. The host recorded it, the model-facing projection showed 16 results, and the tool exposed no next page. The host rejected a missing-input declaration on the strength of the hidden result. Resolve the underlying research requirement once proven, retain failed calls in history, and make every result used to deny a gap retrievable by the worker. ### Medium 7 · Scope and completeness

**Baseline.** The fixture answered FY26 Q3 operating margin with FY25 Q1/Q2 revenue. A year-over-year revenue decline triggered price and consensus checks, while a share rally could pass without a price check. **Checkpoint.** The original unsupported margin case and an FY27 variation were refused. The accounting decline no longer required market evidence. The share-rally case produced a price gap. The dated NVIDIA decision still returned its supported +20% comparison, qualification and two gaps.

**Remaining failures.** “Did the shares fall after the revenue release?” produced a **complete** brief with zero price gaps in both the ordinary MSFT and NVDA fixture paths. The coverage rule recognizes several forms of the verb but misses “fall” here. “What was Q1 revenue in FY25 and Q2 revenue in FY26?” committed two FY25 observations with zero gaps. The adjacent spelling “Q1 FY25 and Q2 FY26” correctly failed. Finally, both “total dividends” and “dividends per share” received the same complete USD 0.61 per-share answer. Each figure had a source. The unsupported parts were the market premise, the requested year, or the aggregate amount. Before accepting complete, bind each requested metric to its own period and unit, and require market evidence whenever an answered premise concerns a share-price move. ### Medium 8 · Watchlist projection

**Baseline.** Qualified CASH plus MSFT rejected the whole snapshot. An embedded synthetic account-like marker was retained in a source label. **Checkpoint.** Both qualified listings were admitted, the marker was rejected before storage, an ordinary benign label worked, and a position-report header still failed without changing active membership. **Remaining failure.** In both builds, an instrument-only snapshot containing `PNL,XLON,STK,GBX` and MSFT was rejected with zero projected entries. Changing only PNL to PCT admitted both rows. PNL is the listed ticker for Personal Assets Trust ordinary shares. The validator gives literal CASH an exception while PNL remains a forbidden report label even in a qualified ticker field. Validate report headers separately from instrument values. This is a valid-input failure. The repaired source-label privacy checks passed.
