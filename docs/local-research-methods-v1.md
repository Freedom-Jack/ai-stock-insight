# Local research methods, version 1

Open **Research → Financial and event research methods** in the private desk. Choose a listing. A saved method report pins the observation IDs, document versions, assumptions and arithmetic used for that interpretation. The JSON export includes the full report. A report’s prior revision remains accessible after a new interpretation.

| Method version | Applicable question and inputs | Result, stopping point and reopening trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dossier.v1` | What do saved sources establish about this listing? Read verified issuer financials and dated, sourced company relationships. | Report available measures, a comparable period change and a ranked agenda. Record missing business, segment or geography detail. Reopen after a new filing, operating update or supported relationship. |
| `earnings_preparation.v1` | What should an upcoming earnings disclosure resolve? Pin an event edition and financial metric, period, units and accounting basis. | Preserve separate management guidance, Street consensus, model estimate and operator-scenario records when available. Each revision points to its predecessor. Check the original release date and subsequent revisions. |
| `earnings_review.v1` | How did the event’s reported result compare with what was knowable beforehand? Read the event’s primary passage, same-document qualification, comparable prior financials and dated expectation history. | Calculate only aligned values frozen before the conservative release cutoff. Late expectations remain identifiable. Unverified consensus, market reactions and missing transcripts remain explicit. Reopen for a corrected release, transcript or guidance. |
| `event_review.v1` | How does this specific issuer or policy event bear on an active listing? Pin the exact calendar edition or published announcement. Investigate disclosure, expectations or documented exposure. | State the event and its precision. Give a mechanism to investigate, a plausible alternative and the evidence that could distinguish them. Keep publication, implementation and financial outcomes distinct. Reopen on a revised calendar, decision, primary release or implementing document. |
| `peer_comparison.v1` | How do two issuers compare on a common reported metric? Use two actuals with aligned fiscal dates, currency, units, duration, accounting basis and share basis. | Export both exact source versions and the absolute and relative differences. Reject mismatches. An effectively zero denominator permits an absolute difference only. Reopen on a restatement or a better-aligned peer. |
| `valuation_sensitivity.v1` | How sensitive is conditional enterprise value or per-share value to assumed growth and multiples? Use a positive verified revenue or EPS base, its units, an explicit growth assumption and two positive multiples. | Report low, midpoint and high scenarios with the formula. A standalone quarter is annualized at four times the observed figure. EPS retains its reported share basis. Revenue multiples yield enterprise value. Seasonality and assumed multiples limit interpretation. Reopen with annual results or new assumptions. |
| `price_window.v1` | How much did two regular-session closes differ? Pin the listing, dates, series, currency, delay, adjustment basis and vintage. A second listing can serve as a declared benchmark. | Export endpoints, differences and percentages. A comparable benchmark yields a percentage-point spread. Conflicting or missing endpoints leave the move unresolved. Reopen after obtaining the missing declared session or a consistent adjustment vintage. |
| `historical_cohort.v1` | How did a declared group’s subsequent operating outcomes vary? Freeze the active roster, exact baseline and outcome period ends, metric, decision time and eligible baseline IDs before evaluating outcomes. | Retain positive, negative, flat, missing and excluded members. Export individual comparisons, exclusion reasons and equal-weight descriptive statistics by financial basis. Missing outcomes and tiny denominators do not become zero. Re-evaluate an unchanged rule on newly available disclosures. A new definition requires a separate enrollment. |

The local cohort can measure a reported operating metric or a consistent price series. Its close-price window uses the baseline and outcome session dates. A present-day watchlist can introduce survivorship bias when an operator reconstructs an earlier period. A source must be available by the stated cutoff to qualify for baseline selection.

For closing prices, open **Import market-only closing prices** on the methods page, enter the publisher and series, and paste up to 100 regular-session observations for the selected listing. The form supplies the exact CSV header. Use source timestamps with offsets, an explicit delay in seconds and one of `unadjusted`, `split_adjusted` or `total_return_adjusted`. Adjusted prices require the common adjustment vintage. The desk retains the selected fields and hash of the submitted CSV. It labels this input as operator-supplied and does not verify an upstream market-data license or live endpoint. An imported historical file is available to this workspace from its actual capture time.

For a fixture workspace with the resolved NVDA listing, this synthetic file produces a 10% unadjusted close-to-close increase. Use **Measure a market-session price window** with the dates shown.


The expectation form uses an existing sourced financial observation for its typed units, period shape, share basis and accounting basis. Entering a scenario amount creates an operator-authored estimate. It does not turn the source observation into management guidance or Street consensus. A forecast obtained after the event stays visible as a retrospective entry. For a date-only disclosure, the time boundary conservatively uses the beginning of that calendar day at UTC+14.
